About GamerTech Solutions LLC

Company Overview

GamerTech Solutions LLC is a U.S.-registered, owner-operated business focused on custom VR software development. Day-to-day operations are currently conducted remotely from China.

Current Business Stage

We are at an early business-development stage and have not yet received client orders or delivered paid client projects. This website introduces our intended services and project ideas.

Our Aim

To develop practical VR training, simulation and visualization software with clear requirements and an agreed delivery scope.

Operations & Target Markets

• Legal registration: United States
• Current day-to-day operations: China, working remotely
• Planned target market: United States and other markets where services can lawfully be provided
• Current client activity: seeking our first client projects

Planned Project Approach

For a future engagement, we would agree the requirements, milestones, communication process, testing criteria and handover materials with the client. Any maintenance or support obligations would be defined in that project's agreement.
